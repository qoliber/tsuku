=== Magento-Style Widget Syntax ===

1. Product List Widget:
<div class="widget-product-list">
    <h3>Products in category: Electronics</h3>
    <p>Showing 10 products</p>
    <!-- Product list would be dynamically loaded here -->
</div>

2. Banner Widget:
<div class="widget-banner">
    <a href="/sale">
        <img src="/images/summer.jpg" alt="Summer Sale">
    </a>
</div>

3. Newsletter Widget:
<div class="widget-newsletter">
    <h4>Stay Updated</h4>
    <form method="post" action="/newsletter/subscribe">
        <input type="email" name="email" placeholder="Your email" required>
        <button type="submit">Join Now</button>
    </form>
</div>

4. Conditional Widget Rendering:

<div class="sidebar">
<div class="widget-newsletter">
    <h4>Subscribe Now</h4>
    <form method="post" action="/newsletter/subscribe">
        <input type="email" name="email" placeholder="Your email" required>
        <button type="submit">Subscribe</button>
    </form>
</div>

<div class="widget-product-list">
    <h3>Products in category: Electronics</h3>
    <p>Showing 5 products</p>
    <!-- Product list would be dynamically loaded here -->
</div>
</div>


5. Custom Directive for Widget (Magento-style):


<header>
    <div class="promo">Free Shipping on Orders Over $50!</div>
</header>

<main>
    <!-- Main content here -->
</main>

<aside>
    <div class="ad"><img src="/ads/special-offer.jpg"></div>
</aside>

<footer>
    <div class="footer-info">© 2024 My Store. All rights reserved.</div>
</footer>


=== Summary ===
Custom functions allow you to:
1. Create widget-like syntax similar to Magento
2. Parse complex attribute strings
3. Render dynamic components
4. Integrate with your application logic
5. Create reusable template components
